12. Sequential segment playback
One pending utterance, AtomicReference ownership for cross-thread callbacks.
Each segment/chunk awaits completion before the next call.
IDs contain generation + segment index + chunk index.
UtteranceProgressListener maps onDone/onError/onStop to typed completion.

Logical segment over native getMaxSpeechInputLength is split into exact chunks,
same language and voice, without splitting surrogate pairs.
No WAV concatenation, parallel segment playback or files.

[UtteranceProgressListener](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/speech/tts/UtteranceProgressListener).
